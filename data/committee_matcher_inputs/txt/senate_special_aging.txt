Special Committee on Aging

Source authority: S. Res. 4, 95th Cong. (1977), Sec. 104(b)(1), Committee System Reorganization Amendments.

It shall be the duty of the Special Committee on Aging to conduct a continuing study of any and all matters pertaining to problems and opportunities of older people, including but not limited to problems and opportunities of maintaining health, of assuring adequate income, of finding employment, of engaging in productive and rewarding activity, of securing proper housing, and, when necessary, of obtaining care or assistance. No proposed legislation is referred to the Committee, and the Committee has no power to report by bill or otherwise exercise legislative jurisdiction; it studies issues, conducts oversight of federal programs, holds hearings, and reports its findings and recommendations to the Senate. In practice its work centers on Medicare and Medicare Advantage, Medicaid, Social Security and retirement income security, pensions, long-term care and nursing homes, family caregiving, prescription drug pricing and access, assisted living and senior housing, employment of older workers, and fraud and scams targeting seniors.
