# Committee jurisdiction


1. Agricultural economics and research.
2. Agricultural extension services and experiment stations.
3. Agricultural production, marketing, and stabilization of prices.
4. Agriculture and agricultural commodities.
5. Animal industry and diseases.
6. Crop insurance and soil conservation.
7. Farm credit and farm security.
8. Food from fresh waters.
9. Food stamp programs.
10. Forestry, and forest reserves and wilderness areas other than those created from the public domain.
11. Home economics.
12. Human nutrition.
13. Inspection of livestock, meat, and agricultural products.
14. Pests and pesticides.
15. Plant industry, soils, and agricultural engineering.
16. Rural development, rural electrification, and watersheds.
17. School nutrition programs.

> *Zusatz:* Das Committee studiert und überprüft umfassend Fragen zu Food, Nutrition und Hunger (im In- und Ausland) sowie Rural Affairs.


## Subcommittee on Commodities, Derivatives, Risk Management, and Trade

Oversees commodity programs, derivatives and digital assets, crop insurance, and agricultural trade.
Related Agencies: Farm Service Agency; Foreign Agricultural Service; and the Risk Management Agency at USDA; Commodity Futures Trading Commission

## Subcommittee on Rural Development, Energy, and Credit

Oversees rural development loan and grant programs, renewable energy, and farm loan programs.
Related Agencies: Farm Service Agency (loan programs); Rural Business-Cooperative Service; Rural Housing Service (community facilities programs); and the Rural Utilities Service at USDA; Farm Credit Administration

## Subcommittee on Conservation, Forestry, Natural Resources, and Biotechnology

Oversees conservation programs, forest management, natural resources, pesticides, and agricultural biotechnology.
Related Agencies: Natural Resources Conservation Service; Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service; Agricultural Marketing Service; and the United States Forest Service at USDA; Office of Chemical Safety and Pollution Prevention at the Environmental Protection Agency

## Subcommittee on Food and Nutrition, Specialty Crops, Organics, and Research

Oversees food and nutrition assistance, international food assistance, school meals, horticulture, specialty crops, organic production, and research
Related Agencies: Foreign Agricultural Service (international food assistance); Food, Nutrition, and Consumer Services; the Research, Education and Economics mission area; and the Agricultural Marketing Service at USDA

## Subcommittee on Livestock, Dairy, Poultry, and Food Safety

Oversees livestock, poultry and dairy production, marketing, food safety, and security.
Related Agencies: Agencies within the Marketing and Regulatory Program and Food Safety mission areas, and the Office of Homeland Security at USDA
