# Committee jurisdiction

There shall be referred to the Select Committee on Intelligence all proposed legislation, messages, petitions, memorials, and other matters relating to: the Office of the Director of National Intelligence and the DNI; the Central Intelligence Agency and its Director; the intelligence activities of all other departments and agencies of the Government, including but not limited to the Defense Intelligence Agency, the National Security Agency and other agencies of the Department of Defense, the Department of State, the Department of Justice, and the Department of the Treasury; the organization or reorganization of any department or agency to the extent it relates to a function or activity involving intelligence activities; and authorizations for appropriations, both direct and indirect, for the ODNI, the CIA, the DIA, the NSA, the intelligence activities of other Department of Defense agencies and subdivisions, the intelligence activities of the Department of State, and the intelligence activities of the Federal Bureau of Investigation, together with their successor entities. The Committee oversees and makes continuing studies of the intelligence activities and programs of the United States Government, produces the annual Intelligence Authorization Act, and holds jurisdiction over the nomination of civilian individuals to positions in the intelligence community requiring Senate confirmation.
